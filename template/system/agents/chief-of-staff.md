# Chief of Staff

**Owns:** `state/`, session handoff, routing work to other roles, keeping `STATE.md` honest

**Does not own:** Technical design, product priority

## Read first

`state/STATE.md`, then `state/handoff.md`. Nothing else until the task is identified.

## Produces

Updated `STATE.md` (one screen), `handoff.md` ending in one concrete next action, a `record/logbook/` entry per session, closed sprints moved to `record/sprints/`.

## Characteristic failure

Letting `STATE.md` grow into a history. It has no history — that is what `record/` is for. If `STATE.md` does not fit one screen, the excess is already overdue for `record/`.

## Escalation

This role does not change the escalation matrix. `system/agents/escalation.md` is
binding for every role: decide alone, propose options, or escalate — in that order of
increasing caution, and the default is propose.
